# Content required from the artist

Nothing in this list has been invented on the site. Public pages describe only the practice that is already established: Teodor Dukov, Bulgarian sculptor and artist, working in wood, with Theo Apples as the signature sculptural collection. Empty records stay empty.

Mark each item **CONTENT REQUIRED FROM ARTIST** until Teodor confirms it.

## Identity

- Biography in his own wording
- Artist statement
- Education
- CV file
- Studio address, only if it should be public
- Email address for enquiries
- Official social and gallery profile URLs (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, gallery pages). None are linked until the URL is confirmed

## Works

For every sculpture, Theo Apple, furniture piece, painting and project:

- Confirmed title, in English and Bulgarian if the title is translated
- Year
- Materials and the exact surface (pigment, lacquer, gold leaf, silver, palladium, or another finish)
- Dimensions
- Unique work or edition, and the edition size
- Availability: public price, price on request, or private enquiry
- Reference number
- Location, where it may be stated
- Detail photographs and an installation photograph
- A description he is willing to publish

Working titles currently on the site, awaiting confirmation:

- Crimson Gala Apple
- Verdant Leaf Apple
- Gilded Concave Apple

These three are in the collection archive. They are not marked for sale.

## Bodies of work still without records

- Sculpture outside Theo Apples
- Furniture: seating, tables, cabinets, objects, commissions
- Paintings: title, year, medium, dimensions
- Projects: name, year, location, client or institution where publication is allowed, materials, scope, result, photographs
- Whether bitten apples, and which other variations, belong to Theo Apples
- History of the Theo Apples collection, from Teodor

## Exhibitions

For each exhibition: name, year, venue, city, country, solo or group, related artworks, installation images, press, catalogue.

## Press and collections

- Publication, headline, date, country, link, short summary, related work
- Public collections, only with permission
- Collaborations that may be named
- Awards, only if he wants them stated and they can be verified

## Pictures

- Professional portrait approved for the site
- Studio portrait
- Process photographs that can take a true caption, for wood, carving, sanding, pigment, polish, and gold, silver or palladium leaf
- High-resolution artwork files. The current public files are the owned archive images, renamed where they are catalogue works

Filenames for catalogue works:

- `teodor-dukov-crimson-gala-apple-wood-sculpture.png`
- `teodor-dukov-verdant-leaf-apple-wood-sculpture.png`
- `teodor-dukov-gilded-concave-apple-wood-sculpture.png`

Originals remain in `public/images/featured/`. AVIF and WebP derivatives are the next media step, alongside the R2 pipeline. Important images are `<img>` elements with width, height and alt text, not CSS backgrounds.

## Journal

Do not publish a filler article. The first piece should be about a real work, with real photographs, and should link to the collection, the process and the artwork page. One or two such pieces a month is the intended pace.

## What the site will not do

No purchased links, invented press, invented collectors, fake scarcity, or automatic machine translation into further languages.
